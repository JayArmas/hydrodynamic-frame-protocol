# Study evidence outline

Copy the problem specification and enter exact expressions in versioned machine-readable files. For each protocol stage record required inputs, calculation, resulting identities, assumptions, proceed gate, failures, alternatives and saved evidence paths. An empty section is an unresolved obligation.

1. Physical fields, original equations, sources, symmetries, constraints and domain.
2. Entropy divergence, ideal reduction, frame transformations and invariant physical transport.
3. Original matrix, constraint-compatible sectors, shear/simple-sector proof, conservation roots, gaps and principal system.
4. Longitudinal factorization table; at least the physically plausible partitions; exact matching and failed branches.
5. Coefficients, sign/positivity certificates, speed design, denominators and finite symmetry limits.
6. Autonomous row/block calculation if available, invertible transformations, transport compensation and residual freedom.
7. Original determinant and entropy residuals; rest all-real-momentum proof; k=0 gaps; full degree audit.
8. Original boosted matrix/polynomial, laboratory gaps, imaginary-axis and infinity control, all directions and constraints.
9. Every included boundary and intersection; recomputed degrees; marginal modes; separate bounded-evolution and nonlinear obligations.
10. Exact examples, environment/commands/output, proof ledger and hashes.

Do not advance a claim solely because a template has been filled or a numeric sample passes. Link to the actual algebra and analytic argument that closes each gate.
